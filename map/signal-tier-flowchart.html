Overlays

These flags are applied on top of the tier assignment. They don't change a building's tier — they add context.

Reversion Window

Criteria: Hotel building class in PLUTO + HPD shows Class A apartments + zero Class B rooms.

Building was likely converted from hotel to residential. Under the Citywide Hotel Text Amendment, it can revert to transient use without a special permit before Dec 9, 2027.

Prior Operator

Criteria: Building previously operated by a flex-stay company (Sonder, Placemakr, Kasa, Mint House, etc.).

Shows operational viability but does not confirm legal transient capacity. Operator may have left for reasons unrelated to zoning.
Data sources: PLUTO (building class, zoning) and HPD registration (Class A/B unit counts) are the two inputs that determine tier. C of O metadata (issue date, type, dwelling units) is attached as enrichment data after tier assignment — it appears in the detail panel and as a filter, but does not influence the tier. The actual C of O PDF document (which shows floor-by-floor occupancy) is not available programmatically and must be pulled manually from DOB BIS for verification.
