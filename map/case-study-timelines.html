The clearest conversion paper trail. Every stage of the hotel establishment is documented in DOB filings.

A1 (major alteration) filed: "Plumbing work associated with renovation and establishment of existing hotel use within residential building."
First transient signal. "Hotel use" explicitly stated in job description.
Dec 2009 DOB Legacy
Scaffold filings show 133 dwelling units with occupancy code R-1 (transient). Hotel portion is under construction.
R-1 occupancy code confirms transient authorization in DOB.

A1 major alteration: "Renovation and establishment of existing hotel use within residential building." Occupancy changes from RES to J-1 (transient residential, old code).
Definitive conversion filing. Keyword scanning catches "hotel."

Filings for the residential portion show 126 DU with occupancy J-2 (residential, old code). Cellar and lobby renovations. The building now has two distinct occupancy groups side by side.

Rolling Temporary C of Os issued for Job #121469534. DU: 125 → 147. The split-use is authorized but not yet finalized.
2015 DOB Legacy
Residential portion filings update to R-2 occupancy (new code) with 125-147 DU. Transient portion filings continue under R-1.
Apr 2019 ACRIS
$75M assignment/lease recorded. BLDG LIC 39TH ST LLC and related entities assign to Brighthouse Life Insurance Company (MetLife Investment Advisors). Financing event.
Aug 2020 C of O
Final C of O issued for Job #121469534. DU: 192 → 191. The split-use (125 residential + 66 transient) is now formally permanent.
Final C of O. The strongest confirmation of legal transient capacity.
Dec 2023 ACRIS
$90M RPTT sale recorded. Goldman family entities (Deans Management, Lloyd/Katja/Dorian Goldman) to BLDG HOLDINGS II LLC. Ownership restructuring within the same family.
Current DCWP
Sonder The Nash hotel license: Ready for Renewal. Expiration: Sep 30, 2026. Active transient operation.
What the pipeline sees: HPD Class B = 66 (caught). But even without HPD, three other signals would flag this building: DOB R-1 occupancy (2009), keyword "hotel" in DOB filings (2008, 2011), and the DCWP hotel license. The 2008 DOB filing explicitly says "hotel use" — our keyword scanning catches this 12 years before HPD's Class B registration would have been the only signal.

Surrendered license = operator change. Kasa may not have filed a new DCWP license yet.

All recent filings are facade restoration (landmark compliance), sidewalk sheds, and one unit renovation. No change-of-use applications. Transient capacity is stable.
What the pipeline sees: HPD Class B = 165 (strong signal). PLUTO class RM, not hotel. C of O is still only Temporary — the building has been operating under rolling temporaries since at least 2019. The surrendered DCWP license from Mint House is a data point: it confirms the building had a licensed hotel operator, and the surrender corresponds to the Kasa takeover.

Awning installations for Gourmet Garage (ground floor retail tenant). 10 DU listed, occupancy J-1. The building has commercial retail below and transient rooms above.

Temporary C of O issued for Job #110402467 (A1 major alteration). 151 dwelling units.

Final C of O issued for Job #110402467. 151 dwelling units confirmed.
Final C of O. Transient use permanently authorized.
Current HPD
HPD registration: 0 Class A + 151 Class B. DOB building class: "HEREAFTER ERECTED CLASS B." 10 legal stories. 100% transient — no permanent residents.
HPD confirms all 151 units are transient. The DOB building class says Class B.
Current PLUTO
Building class: RC (residential-commercial). Not hotel class. PLUTO reports 0 residential area, 93,148 sq ft commercial area. Owner listed as "UNAVAILABLE OWNER" (condo).
PLUTO misclassifies a 100% transient building. Building class is lagging actual use.
Current DCWP
No hotel license found in the DCWP dataset for this BBL. The Phillips Club may operate under a different license type, or the BBL match may be off due to condo lot structure.
Missing DCWP license. Not all transient buildings have one.
2025 – 2026 ACRIS
Individual condo unit sales active. Recent transactions range from $435K to $1.9M per unit. Units are being bought and sold as investment properties.
2019 – 2025 DOB NOW
All filings are routine maintenance — HVAC, plumbing, sprinkler modifications. Every filing says "no change in use, egress or occupancy." Stable operation.
What the pipeline sees: HPD Class B = 151 catches this building with high confidence. But this case exposes two weaknesses: (1) PLUTO says RC, not hotel — if HPD had missed this building, the pipeline would have classified it as Partial. (2) No DCWP hotel license found, so that signal can't serve as a backup either. The Phillips Club proves that HPD Class B is the most reliable signal, and building class alone is insufficient.

The building HPD missed. Had transient capacity, is now converting out. Shows how a building exits the transient pool.

Pipeline Tier
Partial Signal
HPD Registration
491 Class A · 0 Class B

RM · C5-5 · 31 floors

2011 – 2014 DOB Legacy
Filings show 491-502 DU with occupancy J-2 (residential, old code). Fire alarm, sprinkler, and interior renovation work. Some filings show occupancy "E" (assembly) for commercial portions.
Nov 2015 C of O
First Temporary C of O issued for Job #122153141. DU: 491. The building is operating under temporary authorization.
Dec 2017 DOB Legacy
Lobby renovation filings show occupancy "B" (business/commercial) with 491 DU. Tenant protection plan filed referencing A1 #121203713 and A2 #140664751.
2019 DOB Legacy
Filings now show 735 DU with R-2 (residential) occupancy. The dwelling unit count jumped from 491 to 735 — a 244-unit increase suggesting a portion of the building is being subdivided or reclassified.
DU jump from 491 to 735 is a conversion signal — but in which direction?
2020 DOB Legacy
Some filings show 837 DU with R-2 occupancy. Temporary C of Os for Job #121203713 show DU 491 → 559.
Nov 2020 DOB Legacy
A1 (major alteration): "General construction associated with conversion of a portion of Wing B of existing building to residential use." DU: 491 → 345.
The conversion filing. Wing B is being converted from transient to residential. DU drops because former hotel rooms are being consolidated into larger apartments.
Jan 2021 C of O
Last Temporary C of O on record for Job #121203713. DU: 491 → 559.
Current HPD
HPD registration: 491 Class A + 0 Class B. DOB building class: "COMMERCIAL ALTERED CLASS A."
HPD never registered the transient rooms as Class B. The R-1 portion was only visible in DOB filings.
Current DCWP
Sonder Battery Park hotel license: Ready for Renewal. Expiration: Sep 30, 2026. License is still technically valid despite Sonder closing in July 2026.
DCWP license lags operations. Sonder closed but the license hasn't been surrendered.
2025 – 2026 DOB NOW
Plaza renovation, construction fencing, structural work. No transient-related activity. The building is moving on from its hotel past.
What the pipeline sees: PLUTO class RM (partial signal). HPD 0 Class B (missed). DCWP license still shows Ready for Renewal (lagging). The building's transient history was only visible through: (1) DOB R-1 occupancy in fire alarm filings, (2) the prior operator ground truth, and (3) now the DCWP license. But the 2020 conversion filing confirms this building is leaving the transient pool — the condo board is converting Wing B to residential. A dead end for Kasa, but the case taught us to look beyond HPD.
