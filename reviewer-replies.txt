Both flagged. The counting question mattered more than the thresholds. The Act

    116 John Street    183 Class B, 233 Class A   total would say 416

International House at 492, NYU University Hall at 478. Neither is a hotel.

Fixed. 372 buildings cross 100 rooms, 67 cross 400.

--- TWO THINGS BACK TO YOU ------------------------------------------------

Is use group worth building?

Our read: current use covers the operational question — is this a dorm, a
shelter, an NPISA — and that is now done. Use group covers entitlement, which
matters for a smaller set: buildings where a use change is contemplated, and
manufacturing districts where Use Group 3 sits on a density bonus.

If that is right, we would parse certificates at diligence for specific
buildings rather than build a citywide parser. If you would not trust a
building without its use group on file, say so. That is a different project.

A costed improvement we have not spent on.

Part of that 21% no-signal rate is our own test being too strict. It throws
out anything filed on a cross street — the Wythe Hotel occupies 75 North 11th
Street and registers as 80 Wythe Avenue.

We have built a better test and not run it. Doing so costs about $85 in API
calls. Flagging it so you know the 21% is partly ours, not all real.
